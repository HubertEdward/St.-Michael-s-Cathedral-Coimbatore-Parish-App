# St. Michael's Cathedral, Coimbatore parish app: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This app was made for the parish family of St. Michael's Cathedral, Coimbatore. It is free, has no
advertising and is not sold. The same promise appears inside the app under More → Settings →
Privacy, in Tamil and English (`apps/mobile/src/app/privacy.tsx`). Keep the two saying the same
thing.

This policy describes the **preview version** of the app. It must be updated, and parishioners told
in the app, before the app is connected to the parish office's server (see "When the app is
connected to the parish office" below).

## What the app stores, and where

In this preview, everything is kept **on your phone only**:

- your language and typing choices (Tamil typing on or off, the spellings you picked for words
  typed in English letters, the language to listen for when you speak);
- if you sign in: your phone number and PIN, your family's register entry (family name, head of
  family, address, chosen Anbiyam) and its members: each one's name and relationship to the head
  of the family and, if given, their date of birth, education, what they do now (their class if
  studying; the sector, their work and a yearly income range if working) and the dates of their
  baptism, First Holy Communion, confirmation and marriage;
- Mass intentions and certificate requests you send (the names, dates and notes you type, your name
  and phone number);
- monthly-offering set-ups you make (amount, purpose, debit day, your name and phone number; no money
  moves in the preview);
- the messages of the groups you belong to, and which ones you have read;
- the sample parish data the preview starts with.

The register's questions beyond a name and relationship are all optional. The parish asks them to
know its families and plan its care, for example help with studies or for families in need.
Income is asked only as a range, with "Prefer not to say" as one of the answers.

Nothing is sent to the parish office, to the app's developer or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app
deletes everything it stored. More → Settings → "Reset preview data" clears the preview's details
and starts again.

## When the app is connected to the parish office

A later version will connect the app to a server run for the parish, so that the office can approve
families, answer requests and send messages. When that happens:

- your family register, requests, monthly-offering set-ups and your groups' messages will be stored
  on that server and used only for the life of the parish;
- your family's details will be seen only by the parish priest and the parish office staff he
  authorises;
- the office-bearers of your Anbiyam and of the groups you join will see the names of their own
  members, so they can send them messages;
- your PIN will be stored only in a form that cannot be read back (a one-way hash).

This section will be rewritten with the exact details (where the server is, who runs it, how long
records are kept, how to ask for your details or have them removed) before the connection is
switched on, and the app will tell you before it starts.

## Signing in

You sign in with your phone number and a six-digit PIN that you choose. No SMS or one-time password
is sent, so no SMS service receives your number. The parish office will never ask for your PIN. If
you forget it, the office gives you a one-time code to set a new one.

## What the app does not do

- No advertising.
- No tracking, analytics or usage statistics.
- No access to your contacts, photos, location or camera.
- No sale of data, and no sharing for any purpose other than the life of the parish.

## Permissions

- **Microphone** (optional): used only while you tap the microphone beside a text box to speak
  instead of typing. See "Speaking instead of typing" below.
- **Internet**: used for speech recognition when your phone has no offline speech for the language,
  and, in a later version, to reach the parish office's server.

## Speaking instead of typing

Text boxes have a microphone for saying your intention or message instead of typing it, in Tamil or
English. The app uses the phone's own speech recognition. When the phone has offline speech for the
language (for example Google's offline speech recognition), the words are recognised on the phone
and nothing leaves it. When it does not, the app tells you before the first use that what you say
will be sent to the phone's speech service (Google) to be turned into text, and goes ahead only if
you agree. How that service handles speech is described in its provider's privacy policy. The app
itself keeps no sound, only the text you choose to send.

## Typing Tamil in English letters

Turning English-letter words into Tamil happens on the phone, with a word list built into the app.
The spellings you pick are remembered on your phone only.

## Other apps the parish app opens

Calling the office, directions, email and UPI payments open your phone's own apps (the dialler,
maps, your email app, your UPI app). What you do there is covered by those apps' own privacy
policies. The parish app never sees your bank or card details.

## Children

Parents or guardians enter their children's names and dates of birth as members of their family.

## Questions

Please contact the parish office: St. Michael's Cathedral, Coimbatore, or the parish email shown in
the app under More → Contact.
