{{APP_NAME}} · Parish app demo

The parishioner app and the parish office app, side by side. Changes in one show up in the other.

Try this

  1. Choose a languagePick Tamil or English on the phone. Change it later in More → Language and settings.
  2. Register a familyPhone: the person icon on Home, tap First time here and choose a 6-digit PIN with any number, then Register my family. Office: Families shows the request; approve it, and the phone shows Approved with a family code. Relatives use that code to ask to join, and the office approves them too. Sign in with 98430 20001 and PIN 246810 to see an approved family.
  3. Forgotten PINNo SMS is ever sent. Office: Families, Family directory, Reset PIN gives a one-day code. Phone: Sign in, Forgot your PIN, enter the code and a new PIN.
  4. Request a Mass intentionOn the phone: Mass intention, choose the day and Mass, enter the names, Submit.
  5. Approve it in the officeOffice: Mass intentions. The new request is at the top; approve it, then print the priest's list for that day.
  6. See the answer on the phonePhone: More → My requests shows it as Approved.
  7. Request a certificatePhone: More → Request a certificate. In the office, approve it under Certificates and add a note such as "Collect on Friday"; the note appears in My requests.
  8. Publish an announcementOffice: Announcements → New, with Tamil and English text. It appears at once as a story at the top of the phone's Home; tap it to watch.
  9. Change the timetableOffice: Mass timetable. Add or edit a Mass; the phone's Next Mass and Mass times follow.
  10. Start a monthly offeringPhone: Give. Choose an amount and purpose, Set up UPI AutoPay, Approve. The office sees the new supporter and the monthly total under Monthly offerings. No money moves.
  11. Pray the RosaryPhone: the round Pray button in the middle. Tap for each Hail Mary; the beads fill and the next mystery follows. Then light a candle.
  12. Plan an event and a rosterOffice: Events and Ministry rosters. The phone shows them as stories on Home and under More → Ministry duty rosters.

This is a preview with sample data, kept in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere. Facts about the parish still need the office's confirmation.
